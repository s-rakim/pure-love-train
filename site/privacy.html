You&I

Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

You&I is made by Malcolm, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: rakimmalcolm06@gmail.com.

Your private backup

Your cycle logs (including symptoms, flow and notes), names, anniversary and chosen look live on your phone. A private copy is also kept on our server as a backup, so you can get them back on a new phone. Your partner can't see it. It can only be restored by you signing in, or with a one-time code your partner makes for you (see below).

What we store on our server, and why

To let two phones share one space, our server stores:

  • Your display name, your partner's name, your anniversary and your next date
  • Your moods, daily answers, love notes, hugs, This or that picks, Open when… letters, love coupons and date ideas
  • Photos and videos you send as moments, with their captions
  • Your period dates and cycle predictions, but only while “Share with your partner” is on
  • The private backup described above
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email address, used only to recognise you and restore your data
  • If you sign in with email: your email address and an account ID. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and never reaches our server
  • A random sign-in token for each phone, and when it last synced

Only you and your partner can see what you share. We do not read, sell or rent your content, and we never use it for ads.

Services we use

  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it, to confirm who you are.
  • Google Firebase Authentication, if you sign in with email, to check your password and send password reset emails. See Firebase's privacy information.

Ads

The app doesn't show ads today. If ads are added later, this policy will be updated first. They will never appear on the cycle screens, and no cycle or health data will ever be given to an ad network.

Your partner's restore code

If you lose your phone, your partner can make a one-time code in the app that signs a new phone in as you, with your backup. It works once, for 30 minutes. Anyone holding the code could use it, so only share it with the person it's for.

How long we keep data

We keep your shared content until you delete it. Deleting a moment deletes its photo or video from the server. A phone that hasn't synced for 180 days is signed out.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Erase all data. This deletes everything you shared from our server: moments with their photos and videos, notes, answers, your sign-in link and your backup. If you no longer have the app, see Delete your data.

Security

Data travels between the app and our server over an encrypted connection. Each partner can change only their own entries, and you can sign out a lost phone from Settings.

Children

You&I is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention it in the app.
