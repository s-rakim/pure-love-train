You&I A private app for couples

A little space just for two —

Daily questions, shared moments, love notes and a streak you keep alive together. Only your partner ever sees it.

You&I is a quiet corner of your phone for the two of you. A question to answer every day, the photos you share, notes for each other, and, if you want, a shared view of your cycle.

What's inside

  1. 01

    A question every day

    Answer today's question and see what your partner said. It's a small way to keep learning about each other.

  2. 02

    This or that

    Quick pairs to pick between, fifty a day. Find out where you agree, and where you really don't.

  3. 03

    Moments

    Photos and videos for just the two of you, kept together in one place.

  4. 04

    Notes, letters and coupons

    Leave something for them to find, write a letter, or save date ideas for later.

  5. 05

    Your streak

    Show up for each other every day and keep the flame going together.

  6. 06

    Your cycle, if you want

    Share where you are and what's coming, so your partner knows how to show up.
