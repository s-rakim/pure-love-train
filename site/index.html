You&I

A little space just for two.

You&I is a private app for couples: a daily question, shared moments, love notes, This or that, a streak to keep alive together, and an optional shared view of your cycle.

Everything you share goes only to your partner, through a server we run ourselves. No ads use your content, and nothing is sold.

Make it yours with four themes (Candle, Lilac, Lavender and Misty), each in light and dark, and fonts from your phone's own to a flowing script.
